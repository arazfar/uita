# Work required before any operational consideration

Uita is a bounded research simulator. Passing its verifier means only that a plan passes the implemented rule pack against the available modeled evidence. It does not establish FAA compliance, complete aviation safety, reliable real-world sensing, or controller replacement.

An operational research program would need authorized, current procedure and airport datasets; local facility directives and agreements; applicable surveillance, equipage and wake-category rules; NOTAM/weather/data-quality integration; validated aircraft and surface dynamics; and independent aviation-domain review. Current licensed geometry, obstacle, performance and procedure datasets would need provenance and update controls. The simplified taxi graph, admission region and weather envelope in this release are not operational procedures.

Human-in-the-loop controller studies would need to examine situation awareness, explanation accuracy, coordination, workload, recovery, authority transfer and failure modes. Simulator proxy metrics cannot substitute for these studies. Fuel and acoustic work would require validated aircraft/engine/acoustic models and relevant authorized data; relative noise exposure is not CNEL or measured decibels.

A safety and assurance program would need requirements traceability, hazard analysis, surveillance/communication failure coverage, security and resilience testing, independently verified contingencies and operational envelopes, and controlled change/promotion governance. It would require coordination with the responsible aviation authorities and facility stakeholders to establish the applicable approval and regulatory path. No such approval, certification, or authorized operational connection is represented here.
