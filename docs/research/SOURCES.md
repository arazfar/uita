- [Change 3](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Order/7110.65BB_Chg_3_dtd_7-9-26_FINAL.pdf): effective July 9, 2026. Its changes include position determination and ground movement provisions, incorporating the previously separate taxi notice.
- [N JO 7110.805](https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Notice/2026-02-24_Notice_7110.805_Apchs_to_Multiple_Runways_FINAL_Updated.pdf): effective June 19–December 24, 2026. Revises 7-4-4; closely spaced visual approaches require preceding/succeeding identification, applicable separation before establishment, visual acquisition and maintenance, and overtaking restrictions. Uita does not implement or claim authority for that visual-separation procedure.
- Index date audit: 7110.806 effective September 14, 2026 concerns Micro-EARTS; Uita does not model that equipment. 7110.807 starts October 18, 2026 and 7110.803/804 start October 28; these are future as of this research date. 7110.798 expired July 9 and is incorporated into Change 3. A listed notice is not automatically applicable.
- [Radar arrivals, 5-9](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/atc_html/chap5_section_9.html): separate responsibilities and conditions exist for dependent/independent approaches. Generic dependent-approach geometry cannot be applied to SFO's 750-foot pairs without local authority.
- [Departure/intersecting-runway operations, 3-9](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/atc_html/chap3_section_9.html): occupation/intersection progress and wake conditions govern when another takeoff may begin. Uita's shared reservation resources are a deliberately restrictive research abstraction, not a translation of every exception.

This discovered identifier is the runtime pin. Account discovery confirms `response_format`, `max_tokens`, `max_completion_tokens`, `temperature`, `seed`, 262144-token context, and FP8. Non-secret metadata is retained in `crusoe-capabilities-2026-10-17.json`. Generation verification is recorded separately in the actual live evaluation report. Offline experiments must retain fixture labeling and zero inference-call/cost accounting. Live mode must validate model membership and capabilities with credentials, enforce an approved finite budget and record the returned metadata. Never substitute another provider.
